Cookie Policy & Digital Privacy Preferences

Effective Date: January 1, 2026 • AuraDialVale Privacy Governance

1. Principles of Digital Elegance and Restraint

AuraDialVale operates under a core philosophy of luxury restraint, collector privacy, and digital minimalism. We believe that exploring Haute Horlogerie calibres, celestial dials, and mechanical complications online should be a refined, secure experience unencumbered by invasive third-party ad tracking networks. This Cookie Policy explains transparently how cookies and browser storage technologies function on our website.

2. Strictly Necessary Technical Cookies

Essential technical cookies are required for the fundamental operation, security, and rendering stability of our website. These cookies manage cryptographic SSL/TLS connection states, maintain session integrity across page navigations, protect against automated denial-of-service attacks, and facilitate secure concierge appointment submissions. Essential cookies cannot be disabled without impairing core site security.

3. Local Storage for Lighting Themes and Reader Typography

To enhance your viewing comfort, AuraDialVale utilizes client-side HTML5 Local Storage to remember your selected aesthetic lighting theme (Astral Obsidian, Grand Feu Porcelain, or Aventurine Midnight) and your in-article typography scaling adjustments. This data is saved exclusively inside your local browser sandbox and is never transmitted to external marketing servers.

4. Anonymized Measurement & Performance Analytics

We employ privacy-compliant Google Analytics 4 telemetry (Google Tag `G-0LY0HY7L01`) to analyze aggregate website traffic, popular horology treatises, and device viewport resolutions. All visitor IP addresses are automatically pseudonymized at the point of ingestion, ensuring that no personal identity or collector record is ever linked to your browsing patterns.

5. Browser Management and Cookie Controls

You retain complete control to review, restrict, or clear stored cookies at any time through your web browser settings menu (supported in Chrome, Safari, Firefox, and Edge). Please note that disabling cookies may affect certain dynamic features such as the interactive horology escapement simulator.

6. Data Protection Officer and Atelier Inquiries

If you have questions regarding our digital privacy practices or wish to review your stored preferences, please contact our data governance desk at 181 Mercer Street, New York, NY 10012, United States, or contact us by telephone at +1-888-777-5845.
