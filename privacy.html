Privacy Policy & Client Data Stewardship

Effective Date: January 1, 2026 • AuraDialVale Manufacture Standards

1. Commitment to Collector Privacy and Discretion

At AuraDialVale, we recognize that the acquisition, customization, and manufacture servicing of Haute Horlogerie timepieces represent private client relationships built upon trust, discretion, and confidentiality. This Privacy Policy details the exact operational standards, cryptographic protocols, and data governance practices governing how we collect, process, and safeguard your personal information when you interact with our digital atelier, use our horological simulator, or schedule salon appointments.

2. Categories of Information Collected Across Our Atelier

When you request concierge appointments, commission bespoke timepieces, or register calibre serial numbers for manufacture warranty coverage, we collect standard contact information including your full name, email address, telephone number, and physical mailing address for secure insured courier transport. Technical parameters, including IP addresses and browser viewport characteristics, are gathered via secure session cookies to ensure stable digital rendering.

3. Lawful Basis and Purpose of Horological Data Processing

We process client data strictly to execute purchase agreements, schedule private salon appointments, manage warranty archives, and provide lifetime restoration services for your timepieces. Technical telemetry is analyzed under legitimate interest frameworks to optimize server reliability and protect our website against malicious traffic. We enforce strict data minimization principles, ensuring that no unnecessary personal data is ever collected.

4. Absolute Non-Disclosure & Third-Party Service Provider Governance

AuraDialVale maintains a strict, unyielding policy: we never sell, rent, monetize, or disclose your personal information, purchase histories, or bespoke commission details to commercial third-party marketing brokers or advertising networks. We share necessary operational data exclusively with trusted enterprise partners, including armored courier logistics providers (e.g. Malca-Amit, Ferrari Express) and encrypted payment clearinghouses compliant with PCI-DSS and SOC 2 security standards.

5. Cryptographic Security Standards & Lifetime Calibre Archives

All digital transmissions on AuraDialVale are protected using modern TLS 1.3 encryption protocols, encrypted database storage, and multi-factor authentication controls. We maintain perpetual physical and digital archive ledgers of every individual timepiece movement serial number, case number, and dial configuration to facilitate authentication and factory restoration for future generations.

6. International Privacy Rights & Data Protection Officer Contact

In accordance with GDPR, CCPA, and international data privacy frameworks, you maintain complete rights to inspect, update, export, or request the deletion of your contact records from our active marketing lists. To exercise your privacy rights or discuss our client data stewardship protocols, please contact our Data Protection Officer at 181 Mercer Street, New York, NY 10012, United States, or telephone +1-888-777-5845.
